# The prompt

Seventh game in the harsh-critic-loop series (after
[NEONOID](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonoid),
[NEON MINER](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonminer),
[NEODROID](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neodroid),
[NEON DASH](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neondash),
[NEONLINGS](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neonlings) and
[NEOPOLIS](https://github.com/melvincarvalho/neopolis)). Levels became
theorems, then gods became theorems. This time the proof is adversarial
and the ground itself is a casualty: **duels as theorems.**

```
Build a Scorched Earth tribute at the level of a modern commercial
artillery game. Single-file browser game, zero assets. Original tanks
and arsenal names where they matter — the real Scorched Earth (Wendell
Hicken, 1991) is shareware royalty and revered here. Angle, power, and
per-turn wind; destructible terrain that remembers every crater; tanks
that fall when undermined; an arsenal economy funded by damage; MIRVs
that split at the apex; napalm that flows downhill; dirt that buries.

One owner writes the whole game. Then /loop harsh sub-agent critics: the
three visual lenses plus a SCORCHED-EARTH-FIDELITY critic judging
mechanics against the 1991 canon.

The harness requirement is absolute: physics is proven against closed
form (the parabola to within 2%, the wind by impact ordering), every
weapon mechanism is machine-checked in isolation, and the duels are
theorems — the exact solver must WIN, a futile gunner must LOSE, and a
solver blinded to the wind must LOSE, proving wind compensation is
load-bearing. Grade against the commercial bar, call out fixes that
didn't land in the pixels, loop until plateau, report the honest number.
```
